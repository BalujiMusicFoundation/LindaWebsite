Frankenstein in India

“Doomed, we travelled like diabolic nomads to remote regions of India, testing our music on unsuspecting spectators.”

Winner of the 2018 Elmbridge Literary Competition, Adult Story category.

The Elmbridge Literary Competition was established by The RC Sherriff Trust, an independent arts charity working in the Borough of Elmbridge, together with Elmbridge Borough Council. Previous competitions have covered subjects as varied as Dickens, fantasy, love and short radio plays. In celebration of the 200th anniversary of the publication of Mary Shelley's Frankenstein, the themes for the 2017–18 competition were “Things That Go Bump in the Night” (ages 5–13) and “A Shiver Down the Spine” (ages 14–18 and Adult).
