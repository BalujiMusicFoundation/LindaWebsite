Who she is

One voice, many colours

Linda Shanson — also known as Linda Shanovitch — is a singer, songwriter, author and painter. An anthropologist by training, she has lived and worked in Mexico, India and Paris, and those journeys run through everything she makes.

She is the voice of Jazz Orient / Re-Orient, the author of the children's classic Journey With The Gods and the award-winning story Frankenstein in India, and a painter whose vivid, dreamlike work gives this site its colour.
