Linda
Shanson

Voice, tabla and story, moving between London, India and the Mediterranean.

Also known as Linda Shanovitch, singer-songwriter with Jazz Orient / Re-Orient, published author, and co-founder of the Baluji Music Foundation.

Read the Bio Explore the Music
In brief

An anthropologist by training, an artist by instinct

BSc Anthropology

Lived and worked in Mexico, India and Paris — experience that continues to shape her songs, stories and stagecraft.

Jazz Orient / Re-Orient

Several albums on the ARC Music label, performed across the UK, Europe and Oman. Dubbed “Bjork on Helium” by the London Evening Standard.

Baluji Music Foundation

Co-founder of the registered charity, and manager of The Inner Vision Orchestra — the UK's only ensemble of professional blind musicians.
