Journey with the Gods

Awoken by a magical moon, two children meet the gods and heroes of Indian mythology.

Awoken by the light of a magical moon, Vinita and Deepak embark on an amazing adventure with magnificent gods and heroes from Indian mythology. Illustrated by Anita Chowdry. It is accompanied by beautiful songs.

Republished by Aunty's House · previously published by Mantra Lingua

Featured by world-famous dancer Akram Khan on his website, reading it to his own daughter, Sayuri, as part of his audio series “An Ancient Observer” (sound design by Pete Thomas).

Now a father himself, world-famous dancer and choreographer Akram Khan narrates stories close to his heart to his daughter, Sayuri, sharing them with all children as part of his audio series “An Ancient Observer.” Journey With The Gods was chosen as part of this series, with sound design by Pete Thomas.

An extract

How the story begins

The next time you gaze into the night sky, take a closer look at the moon. If you are very lucky it may appear round and blue. This blue moon is very precious, for on nights when it shines, impossible things happen!

One night, not long ago, a beam from this blue moon shone into a room where two children lay sleeping. It settled on the picture of Ganesha, the elephant-headed god.

Who is welcome in the house Sitting on a little mouse? Ganesha, Ganesha!

— from Journey With The Gods
