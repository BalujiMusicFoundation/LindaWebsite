Music

Jazz Orient & Re-Orient

Since the late 1980s, Linda has recorded and performed as the vocalist of Jazz Orient — known as Re-Orient when signed to the ARC Music label — alongside sitar virtuoso Baluji Shrivastav and multi-instrumentalist Chris Conway. Together they've released seven albums and one single, drawing on Indian classical traditions, jazz improvisation and European folk.

Once Upon a Night

Jazz Orient (aka Re-Orient, when signed to the ARC Music label) hadn't played together for ten years — busy with other projects, plus two years of the pandemic.

Then one evening, by chance, they found they were all in Chris's home city of Leicester — with instruments! So they went out for dinner at a chaat house on Leicester's Golden Mile, and then on to his Aloft Studio. There they improvised five tracks — just like that. No plan or instructions. They just played.

The magic was still there. Their 33-year, seven-album (and one single) association gave each of them an instinct for sensing what the other two might do at certain times. The tracks are in the order in which they were played. Then, as it was getting late, it was time Baluji & Linda had to go.

But the Jazz Orient / Re-Orient journey continues…
